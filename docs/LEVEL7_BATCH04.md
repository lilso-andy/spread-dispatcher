## Decision

Retain fee 2.15 as the best verified incremental margin change. The two previous-fee comparisons reproduced the same service on each matched seed, with gains of EUR270.44 and EUR19.45 from 2.15. Both fees failed l7-b04-a at 83.91%, and the window candidate also failed at 88.37%. The current policy does not satisfy a universal 90% claim. Across eight development pairs the flexible posting window cut expiries from 551 to 254 but cost EUR211.31 and failed one service threshold. The sixteen comparison runs and the two fee checks had zero undelivered orders and zero refused actions.

On the failed control week 11 of the 14 refunded orders shared deadline minute 4080. The first service-recovery decline occurred at minute 5122. Counting completed late orders therefore reacted after this group had already missed its deadline. A possible next experiment would account for outstanding orders competing for the same delivery day when quoting. The EUR16,000 per week target remains unverified.
